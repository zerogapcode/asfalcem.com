FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us.

Don’t see yours? Call us or send a message — we answer the same business day.

Do you handle the permits?+

Yes. We prepare the drawings, submit the permit application, answer plan-review comments and schedule every city inspection. Permit fees are listed separately on your estimate.

Does my remodel need an architect or engineer?+

Anything that changes the structure — removing a load-bearing wall, adding a room or second story, or a new foundation — usually needs stamped drawings. We tell you at the site visit and coordinate the design so you don’t have to hire separately.

What is a topographic or boundary survey, and do I need one?+

A boundary survey marks your property lines; a topographic survey maps grades and elevations. Most cities require one for additions, new homes, fences near lot lines, pools and drainage work.

Do you work with insurance claims?+

Yes. We document the damage, prepare a detailed estimate for your adjuster and can make emergency repairs to prevent further damage while the claim is reviewed.

How long does a kitchen or bathroom remodel take?+

It depends on scope and permit timelines. You’ll get a written schedule with your estimate, and your project manager updates it every week.
