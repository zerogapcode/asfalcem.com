How it works

From first call to final coat.

Four steps, no surprises. You'll know the price, the colors and the schedule before we open a can.

  1. Tell us about your space

    Send a few photos and a short description through the form, by text or by phone.

  2. Walkthrough & estimate

    We measure, check surfaces, talk colors and sheens, then send a written estimate.

  3. Prep & paint

    Protect, repair and prime, then two finish coats with clean cut lines.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We inspect together, touch up anything you point out, and leave the space clean.

Questions

Good to know.

Don't see your question? Call or text and we'll answer it.

How do estimates work?

Estimates are free. For most projects we visit in person, measure, and send a written estimate within 48 hours. Small interior jobs can often be quoted from photos.

Which sheen should I choose?

It depends on the room and how much cleaning the surface needs. Our usual recommendation:

SheenBest for
Flat / MatteCeilings, low-traffic rooms, hiding wall imperfections
EggshellLiving rooms, bedrooms, hallways
SatinKitchens, bathrooms, kids' rooms, exterior siding
Semi-glossTrim, doors, cabinets
Can you do interior and exterior in one project?

Yes. We can schedule both together and plan the exterior around the weather so your project keeps moving.

How is pricing determined?

Price depends on square footage, surface condition, the amount of prep and repair, number of colors, and the paint line you choose. Your estimate lists each item.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture, protect floors and fixtures, and put everything back when we're done.
